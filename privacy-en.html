Privacy Policy

Sudoku Compass
Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

The current version of the app does not provide features for users to enter or register their names, email addresses, or location information. We have not intentionally integrated advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The app stores game progress, play history, hint usage, and preferences locally on your device to provide game features and resume interrupted games. The app does not automatically transmit this information to a developer-operated server.

3. Transmission to Third Parties

The current version does not include a mechanism to automatically send play history or similar data to the developer. Information collected independently by the operating system or distribution platform may be governed by their respective privacy policies.

4. Deleting Information

Locally stored app data can generally be removed by deleting the app from your device. Data retained in device backups is subject to your device settings and operating system behavior.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to actively collect personal information from users, including children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce advertising, analytics, online features, or other relevant functionality, we will update this policy to reflect those changes.
